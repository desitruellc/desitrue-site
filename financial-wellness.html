Questions

Good to know

Is this financial advice?

It's financial education and coaching: help understanding how money works and what your options are, so you can make your own informed decisions. It isn't investment, legal or tax advice for your situation, and there are no products to sell you.

Do I need to have my finances in order first?

Not at all. Many people come to us because things feel messy. A rough sense of your income and bills is plenty to begin.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. What you share stays between us, always.

Can you run a workshop for my group?

Yes. Get in touch with the size of your group, the topics you're interested in and whether you'd like to meet online or in person, and we'll put something together.

Ready to feel more confident about money?

Start with a free, no-pressure call. Tell us what's on your mind and we'll suggest a first step.
