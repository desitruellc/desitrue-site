The story

Why DésiTrue

I have more than 15 years of experience in full-cycle bookkeeping, accounting, payroll, financial reporting, account reconciliations and operations. I've supported small businesses, professional service firms, management teams and mission-driven organizations with accurate, reliable reporting, secure documentation, efficient workflows and confidential handling, all delivered remotely.

The name comes from mine: Dési from Désirée, True from Truesdale. It's also the promise. Honest numbers, honest advice, and work done the way I said it would be.

Most of my career has been remote, and I love what that makes possible: full focus on the work, without commutes, travel or time zones getting in the way. Working virtually opened my calendar to clients across states and time zones nationwide, and let me take on work by the project rather than by the role, which brings more variety to every engagement.

Today DésiTrue brings bookkeeping, administrative support and creative marketing under one roof, so clients can hand over the work that drains them to one person they trust.
