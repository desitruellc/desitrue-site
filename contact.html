Before you book

Is the first call really free?

Yes. It's a short conversation to understand what you need and whether we're a good fit. No obligation.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Most work happens online, with calls and secure file sharing, so where you live doesn't matter.

How is pricing set?

After the first call you get a plain-language quote, either a monthly package or a fixed price for a project. You'll know the cost before any work starts.

What should I have ready?

Nothing formal. A rough idea of what's taking your time is plenty. For bookkeeping or tax work, recent statements help, and we'll tell you exactly what we need.
