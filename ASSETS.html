DesiTrue photo & logo prompts

Ready-to-paste prompts for an AI image tool such as ChatGPT, Midjourney or Adobe Firefly. Every prompt says exactly which file it becomes, so a finished image drops straight into the site.

How to add a finished image.
  1. Download the image and crop it to the shape listed (for example 4:5).
  2. Resize it to the width listed, then shrink the file at squoosh.app (choose JPG, quality around 75). Aim for under 300 KB.
  3. Name it exactly as shown (for example hero.jpg) and put it in the assets/img folder. The site picks it up automatically, with no code to change.

Logo

The site uses a simple "D" badge and the name in an elegant serif typeface until a real logo is ready. Try these three directions and pick the one that feels most like her.

Before using an AI logo everywhere: AI tools make a picture of a logo, not a proper logo file. Once she picks a favourite, have it redrawn as a vector file (SVG) by a designer (Fiverr or 99designs, often under $50) so it stays sharp on business cards and signs. AI tools also misspell words, so check that "DesiTrue" is spelled exactly right.

Direction 1: Elegant wordmark

Logo design for "DesiTrue", a boutique bookkeeping and business support company. An elegant high-contrast serif wordmark with the exact spelling "DesiTrue", a capital D and a capital T. Rose-gold metallic foil on a plain cream background. Refined, warm, trustworthy, feminine without being frilly. Flat vector style, centred, generous spacing around it, no other text, no mockup.

Direction 2: DT monogram

Monogram logo combining the letters "D" and "T" into one elegant interlocking mark inside a thin circle, for a boutique financial and business support company called DesiTrue. Rose-gold gradient line art on a cream background. Minimal, balanced and timeless, and it must work at small sizes such as a website favicon. Flat vector style, no extra text, no mockup.

Direction 3: Wordmark with a small symbol

Logo for "DesiTrue" (exact spelling): a modern serif wordmark next to a small simple symbol that suggests balance and care, such as a minimal checkmark woven into a leaf, or a balanced scale drawn with one continuous line. Deep rose (#8A4A56) and rose gold (#B76E79) on cream (#FFFAF7). Professional, warm and approachable. Flat vector style, horizontal layout, no tagline, no mockup.
